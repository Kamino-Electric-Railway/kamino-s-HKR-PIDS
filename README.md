# Introduction
This PIDS has two versions, the regular version is for the platform, and the other version is for the lobby or other places.
## Regular version
1.If the train is arriving in 30 minutes, the real time will be shown. If it’s more than 90 minutes away or there’s no train, it will show 'No train service'.
2.'Arriving' starts showing one minute before the train arrives
3.It will show the next two parking stops on a map.
4.It will give a warning 10 seconds before the door closes.
## Other version
1.If the train is arriving in 30 minutes, the real time will be shown. If it’s more than 90 minutes away or there’s no train, it will show 'No train service'.
2.It will show the upcoming stop
